# kalby.overview

**EU AI Act Compliance Matrix** — an interactive, high-level overview of which articles of the EU AI Act apply to each operator.

- **Operators:** providers, deployers, product manufacturers, authorised representatives, importers, distributors
- **Classes:** high-risk AI systems, AI systems, general-purpose AI models
- **Includes:** one-line summaries, application dates, conditions, and changes made by the Digital Omnibus on AI

## Legal basis

- [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 — AI Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj)
- [Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 — Digital Omnibus on AI](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1744/oj/eng) (OJ 24 July 2026, in force 27 July 2026)

## Use

A single self-contained `index.html`, with no build step. Open it locally or serve it with GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch `main`, root).

Matrix data lives in the `R` array in `index.html`, one row per article.

## Disclaimer

General overview, not legal advice.
